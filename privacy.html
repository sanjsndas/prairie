Our privacy commitment

Prairie Biscuit Bloom (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects the privacy of every visitor. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you interact with our journal, whether you're reading essays, subscribing, or sending us a note.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the data you voluntarily provide:

  • Contact form: name, email address, and any message you send.
  • Newsletter sign-up: email address (we use a double opt-in).
  • Usage data: anonymized analytics (via our own light-touch logging) to understand which articles resonate.

2. How we use your information

Your data helps us:

  • Respond to your inquiries and letters.
  • Send occasional updates about new essays, events, or prairie dispatches (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • Improve our content and reader experience.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Ever.

3. Data storage & security

We host our website on secure servers and take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We treat your data with the same care we'd give a handwritten letter.

4. Cookies & tracking

We use minimal cookies — only for functional purposes (like remembering your cookie preferences). We do not use tracking cookies for advertising. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. Simply contact us at privacy@prairiebiscuitbloom.com and we’ll respond within 10 days.

6. Third-party links

Occasionally we link to external resources (e.g., recommended books). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Please review their policies.

7. Children’s privacy

Our journal is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Updates to this policy

We may revise this privacy policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted here, with the effective date noted.
