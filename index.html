    How to start a prairie journal

    A step‑by‑step guide to observing, recording, and learning from the land. No prior experience needed.

    🌱 Step 1: Choose your quadrant

    Pick a 10‑foot square of untended ground. Visit it weekly. Note every living thing.

    ❓ FAQ — common mistakes

    How do I identify plants? Use a hand lens and a local guide. Start with the “Grasses of the Northern Plains” — we have a resource list in our archive.
    Is it okay to pick flowers? No. We advocate for observation without disturbance. Sketch or photograph instead.
    What if I miss a week? That’s part of the practice. Note the gap — it tells a story about your own season.
