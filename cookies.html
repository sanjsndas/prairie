Our cookie philosophy

Prairie Biscuit Bloom (“we”, “us”, “our”) believes in a simple, respectful web. We use cookies sparingly — only to make your visit functional and pleasant, never to profile or track you across the internet.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They're widely used to make sites work more efficiently and to provide useful information to site owners. We use them minimally and transparently.

2. The cookies we use

We use only these essential and functional cookies:

  • Preference cookies: to remember your cookie consent choice (so we don't ask you every time).
  • Session cookies: to keep you logged in (if you have an account) and to maintain a smooth reading experience.
  • Performance cookies: lightweight, anonymized analytics to understand which articles are most read. No personal data is collected.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party marketing cookies.

3. Your cookie preferences

You can manage your cookie preferences below. Your choice will be remembered for 12 months.

4. How to manage cookies in your browser

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • Delete all cookies already stored on your device.
  • Block all cookies (though some sites may not function properly).
  • Set preferences for specific websites.

For more information, visit the help pages of your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).

5. Third-party cookies

We do not use third-party cookies. Any links to external resources (books, articles) are provided for reference and do not set cookies on our behalf.

6. Updates to this policy

We may update this cookie policy occasionally. The latest version will always be posted here, with the effective date noted.
